Proposal · AOA Access 2.0

An identity strong enough to move 70% of a business.

AOA Access 2.0 isn't a feature update — it's a request for every office and rep to change how they've always worked. That only happens if the portal earns trust before it earns a login.

70%

of incoming units, migrated — the number this entire program is built around.

Objective

Zimetrics to support AOA in launching its new customer portal with a cohesive brand identity, an intuitive login and help experience, and a well-orchestrated launch communication program.

Beyond the brief, we recommend
  • Locking the launch date early
  • Segmenting messages for new versus migrating users
  • Designing to accessibility standards
  • Tracking success metrics after launch
What we're launching

AOA Access 2.0, as it stands today

The product is built. Our work is the layer around it: the identity it carries, the first screen people see, the messages that bring them in and the help that keeps them there.
